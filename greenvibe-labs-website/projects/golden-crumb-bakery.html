Our story

We just let the dough take its time

Golden Crumb started with a sourdough starter, a borrowed oven, and the stubborn belief that good bread can't be rushed. Today the oven is our own, but the rule hasn't changed: nothing leaves the kitchen until it's ready.

Every loaf ferments for a full day. Every croissant gets its butter folded in by hand. Whether you're grabbing a Tuesday baguette or planning a wedding cake, you're getting the same care and the same slightly obsessive attention to crust.

Questions, answered

Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs, tree nuts, and sesame every day, so we can't guarantee anything is allergen-free. Tell us your needs when you order and we'll walk you through ingredients and cross-contact honestly.

Custom cakes need at least 3 days' notice, and tiered or wedding cakes need 3 weeks or more. Bread and pastry pre-orders are best placed by the day before pickup.

Not routinely. We focus on pickup so everything stays fresh. For large events, ask us about a one-off delivery and we'll see what's possible.

Come to 24 Maple Lane during open hours on your pickup day and give us your name at the counter. Your order will be boxed and waiting. Cakes are packed in a sturdy box, so keep it flat in the car.

Yes. Share your theme, flavors, and guest count in the form and we'll reply with ideas and a quote. We love florals, simple lettering, and anything slightly whimsical.
